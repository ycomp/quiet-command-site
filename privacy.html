Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Quiet Command ("the app") is a desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what the app does with information from TikTok and YouTube.

What we collect

  • From TikTok, when you connect your account: your basic profile (open ID, display name and avatar), and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues.
  • Videos you choose to post: the video file, its caption and the privacy setting you pick.

How we use it

  • To show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To upload the videos you approve to that account, and to check whether each upload finished.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on your own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted with your operating system's protection. The app has no servers of its own, does not sell or share your data, and sends it only to TikTok (and YouTube, for YouTube uploads) to do what you asked.

Your choices

You can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove the app's access in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page is updated with a new date. Questions: github.com/ycomp/quiet-command-site/issues
